Reflection

For this assignment, I feel like there weren’t really any big messes; I mainly experimented with the durations and different types of transforms. I found out that I mainly like the rotate transform, with most of my stars having the rotate tag. I especially like the skew and translate together, although I think I’d only use them for stars or anything similar if I were to use them again. Despite one of my favorites from the assignment having skew, I think skew is my least favorite. Other than the skew-translate combination, I don’t see myself actually using it. I ideally wouldn’t really want to warp any aspect of my page (at least from where I’m at now), so I think I’d rather use any of the other transforms. For the transition duration, I put all of them at five seconds. I think it’s a good middle ground in general, and I’d probably use five seconds as a good starting point when using this type of code. My main issue from the project, and one I still really haven’t figured out, was how to add this reflection without overlap. I think I managed to fix it in full screen only, but I couldn’t figure out how to fix it for different screen sizes. Instead, I just changed the positioning of the stars to fix it for now.
