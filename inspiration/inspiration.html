These are three artists who inspire me in my work:


Aumipon

For my first artist, I chose Aumipon. Aumipon is an artist I found on TikTok and is known for her clay works, like the bento boxes shown in the image below. I chose her because I love her color schemes and overall simplistic style. Her "trademark" style is always adding a simple, normally monotone face to most of her pieces. I also chose her because she was the artist who got me back into clay and sculpting again.

Yuka Morii

The next artist I chose is Yuka Morii, she's best known for her clay models of Pokémon in the Pokémon card game. I like her style because even if I mainly know her for recreating Pokémon, her overall style is pretty simple, and her designs themselves are actually pretty small. I love how, no matter what she's sculpting, all of her pieces carry a happy and simple attitude with them.

Saya Tsuruta

My final artist is also an artist for the Pokémon card game, Saya Tsuruta. Out of these three, she's definitely the newest one I've been following. Overall, I love her almost crayon-like style and how round and soft her art style is. I started collecting her art because in almost every Pokémon piece she hides a hidden Poké Ball, and I absolutely loved that idea and how clever she was to start this so many years back.
