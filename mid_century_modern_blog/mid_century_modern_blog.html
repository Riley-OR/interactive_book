Reflection

This assignment was definitely more complicated; since there was no CSS outline, it felt like a lot more work, and I definitely made a few mistakes and errors many times throughout the process. Overall, I think I did pretty well on the layout, sans the images and the footer. I couldn’t figure out how to have the footer color go across the page sides, so I left it be. As for the images, I was working on the assignment split screen for the entire time, so I was basing it off of that. Some image sizing and orientations are a bit wonky because of that, but I don’t think it’s that horrendous. Other than that, I had a few errors in the class tags and in making sure all attributes applied, but after a few attempts, I got it all to work out. I did also change the header links to match my others better, and after that I think later on I’ll also change the other headers to match, such as changing ‘point of entry’ to ‘home’ and to organize my point of entry a lot more, probably move around the links so they’re not all jumbled together at the top.
