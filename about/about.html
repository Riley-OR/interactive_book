Biography

Hi, my name is Riley, and if you didn't watch the video, I'm currently a second-year at San Francisco State University and am majoring in Visual Communication Design. I've always been interested in design and anything creative as a whole since I was a kid, and that interest has only amplified since then.


One of my biggest obstacles during my design process, and honestly, an obstacle in my life as a whole, is that I have aphantasia. Aphantasia is the inability to create voluntary images in my mind, which I'm sure you can imagine (I can't) is hard when the entire career I want to pursue is focused on drafts and how things look. Since I was a kid, I've never had a "visual" imagination; I can't even imagine words; I just think it. Even though I see it as an obstacle most of the time, I technically don't know what I'm missing out on. I've seen diagrams of what your mind is supposed to look like, but the idea of actually seeing things in your mind seems so fake to me. Even so, I get by by making the most horrendous drafts and hope that the final draft will end up being what I hope I would imagine.
